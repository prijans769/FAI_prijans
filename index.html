Sub-Task 1: Problem, Objective & Requirements

Problem: Rapid spread of fabricated news and sensationalized clickbait on social platforms erodes public trust and misleads readers.
Objective: Develop an interpretable NLP classifier that scores article credibility (0–100%), flags clickbait tactics, and explains predictions via token weights.
Requirements: Real-time text preprocessing, TF-IDF bigram vectorization, stylometric feature extraction, and interactive confusion matrix evaluation.

Sub-Task 2: AI Development & Architecture

Pipeline: Text Normalization → Stop-word Filtering → TF-IDF Vectorizer (1–2 n-grams) + Stylometric Extractor (CAPS ratio, punctuation density, attribution cues) → L2-Regularized Logistic Regression.
Explainability: Exposes log-odds weights directly in the UI so users see why an article was flagged rather than receiving a black-box score.

Sub-Task 3: Testing & Quantitative Results

Dataset: 20,000 labeled articles (80/20 train-test split, 4,000 test samples).
v1.0 Baseline: 88.2% Accuracy, 0.879 F1-Score.
v2.0 Tuned Model: 94.8% Accuracy, 95.2% Precision, 94.3% Recall, 0.984 ROC-AUC after adding bigrams and source attribution markers.

Sub-Task 4: Portfolio & Presentation Readiness

Deliverables Included: Interactive Live Analyzer web app, token-level trigger highlighter, interactive Confusion Matrix & ROC curve benchmark dashboard, and reproducible feature-weight diagnostics suitable for live classroom demonstration.
